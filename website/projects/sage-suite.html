The brief

A bedroom built around one calm colour.

The brief was a master bedroom that felt restful the moment you walk in: soft to the eye, soft to the touch and free of clutter.

A full-height wall of upholstered sage panels sets the tone, framed by warm brass lights. Fluted timber wraps the dressing corner, where an arched mirror glows from behind, and the media wall is kept low and quiet so the bed stays the focus.

Design note

“One colour, three textures, nothing extra.”

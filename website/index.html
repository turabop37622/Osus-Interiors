Questions

Good to know
before we begin.

How much does an interior project cost?

Every home is different, so we price each project after the first site visit. You receive a clear, itemised estimate—design fees, materials, furniture and execution listed separately—before any work begins, with options to suit different budgets.

How long does a project take?

A single room usually takes 4–8 weeks, a complete apartment 3–4 months and a full house 6–12 months, depending on construction work and custom furniture. We share a week-by-week schedule at the start and keep you updated throughout.

Which cities do you work in?

Our team works on site across Lahore and Islamabad. For other cities in Pakistan and clients abroad, we offer design-only packages with video consultations and detailed drawings your local contractor can follow.

Do you handle construction and furniture too?

Yes. Our turnkey service covers civil and structural changes, ceilings, flooring, custom joinery, lighting, furniture and final styling—one team, one point of contact, from empty site to a home that is ready to live in.

What happens in the first consultation?

The first consultation is free and takes about an hour, on site or by video. We talk about how you live, your budget and timeline. Bring floor plans or photos of the space and any images that inspire you.
